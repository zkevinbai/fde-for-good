# For partners (mission-driven organizations & small businesses)

You have something technical you need built, and no realistic way to pay for it. That's exactly what we're here for.

This is for you whether you're a **mission-driven organization** — a charity, community group, or social enterprise — or a **small local business**, including for-profit ones: the café with no website, the shop still taking bookings by phone, the one-person trade drowning in spreadsheets.

First, a quick check: see [who we help](who-we-help.md) to make sure it's a fit. The short version — we help people who are locked out of software because they can't afford it.

## What we do

Volunteer engineers build software for you, for free, and keep it open source. Think:

- A first **website** or online presence
- A **booking, ordering, or enquiry** system
- **Automating** manual, repetitive work (the stuff eating your evenings)
- Turning messy **spreadsheets and data** into something usable
- A small **internal tool** or dashboard
- **Connecting systems** that don't currently talk to each other

## What we ask of you

The work goes much better when someone on your side can:

- Spend a few hours showing us how things work today
- Be available for occasional questions and quick demos
- Tell us honestly what "good" looks like for you

We embed with you — we don't disappear and reappear with something that misses the point.

## What it costs

Nothing in fees. Two things to know:

- The **code** we write is open source (MIT) and shared publicly. **Your data is not** — see [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md).
- We don't take on costs you can't sustain. If something needs ongoing paid hosting, we'll be upfront and keep it as cheap (often free) as possible, in your name.

## How to start

The easiest way is the **Request help** form on our website — no GitHub account or tech knowledge needed. (If you'd rather, you can open a GitHub issue with the "Request help" template instead.) Either way, tell us:

- Who you are and what you do
- The problem, in your own words — what's painful today
- Whether you can afford to pay for this elsewhere (be honest — it helps us prioritize fairly)
- Who'd be our point of contact

We'll follow up to scope it together.
