# For volunteer engineers

You want to use your skills for something good. Here's how to plug in.

## What an FDE does here

A forward-deployed engineer embeds with an organization — a mission-driven organization or a small local business — learns their real workflow, and ships software that fits. You're not just taking a ticket; you're figuring out what's actually worth building, then building it.

You don't need to be senior, and you don't need a specific stack. Curiosity, follow-through, and the willingness to talk to non-technical people matter more. (Sometimes the most valuable thing you can build is someone's very first website.)

Before taking something on, it's worth a glance at [who we help](who-we-help.md) so you know the kind of work that's in scope.

## Finding work

1. Browse open engagements in [`clients/`](../clients/) and the issue tracker.
2. Look for projects marked `proposed` or `scoping`, or claim a fresh `Request help` issue.
3. Comment to claim it so we don't double up.

## Starting a new engagement

1. Copy the templates from [`clients/_template-client/`](../clients/_template-client/):
   - `clients/<client-name>/README.md`
   - `clients/<client-name>/projects/<project-name>/README.md`
2. Fill them in during scoping.
3. Build inside `projects/<project-name>/` — it's self-contained, bring whatever stack fits.

## How to be a good FDE

- **Scope small.** Ship something useful, then do the next thing. A finished small tool beats an abandoned big one.
- **Stay close to the org.** Demo early and often. Build *with* them.
- **Document as you go.** The handover is part of the job, not an afterthought.
- **Protect their data.** Read [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md) before you touch anything real.
- **Make it reusable.** If another org could use what you built, write the README so they can.

## Handover checklist

- [ ] README explains how to run / use it
- [ ] Accounts, deploys, and credentials handed to the org
- [ ] Walkthrough done with the people who'll use it
- [ ] Honest notes on what's fragile or unfinished
- [ ] Project status updated to `done`
