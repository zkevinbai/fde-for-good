# For charities & nonprofits

You have a technical problem and no budget to solve it. That's exactly what we're here for.

## What we do

Volunteer engineers build software for you, for free, and keep it open source. Think:

- Automating manual, repetitive work
- Turning messy spreadsheets and data into something usable
- Building a small internal tool, dashboard, or website
- Connecting systems that don't currently talk to each other

## What we ask of you

The work goes much better when someone on your side can:

- Spend a few hours explaining how things work today
- Be available for occasional questions and quick demos
- Help us understand what "good" looks like for your team

We embed with you — we don't disappear and reappear with something that misses the point.

## What it costs

Nothing in fees. The only thing to know: the **code** we write is open source (MIT). The code is shared publicly; **your data is not** — see [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md) for how we keep sensitive information out of the open.

## How to start

Open a request as a GitHub issue using the **"Charity request"** template, or email a maintainer if that's easier. Tell us:

- Who you are and what your mission is
- The problem, in your own words — what's painful today
- Roughly who'd be our point of contact

We'll follow up to scope it together.
