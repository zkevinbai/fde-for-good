How it works

From "this is painful" to shipped

Five steps, every time. Small enough to finish, open the whole way through.

01

Intake

You tell us what's slow, manual, or impossible today — in your own words.

02

Scope

We shape it together into a project small enough to actually finish.

03

Build

Engineers embed and ship in the open, demoing early and often.

04

Handover

Docs, accounts, and a walkthrough — you fully own what we built.

05

Stay open

The code stays public for the next org with the very same problem.

Who we help

People locked out of software

The test isn't whether you make money — it's whether you could get this any other way.

Mission-driven organizations

Charities, nonprofits, community groups, mutual aid, and social enterprises — the people quietly holding things together.

  • ✓ Real impact for the people you serve
  • ✓ Your data stays private; the code stays open
  • ✓ Tools you can run without a tech team

Small local businesses

The family café, the corner shop, the one-person trade. For-profit is completely fine — what matters is you're small, with no engineer and no budget to hire one.

  • ✓ A first website or online presence
  • ✓ Booking, ordering, or enquiry systems
  • ✓ Automate the work eating your evenings

Where we're headed

Today a collective.
Tomorrow a nonprofit.

FDE for Good starts as volunteers. The goal is to grow into a registered nonprofit in its own right — so we can take donations, sustain the work, and back our volunteers and partners properly.

Collective → Fiscal sponsorship → Incorporate → Registered nonprofit
