# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are a community of volunteers helping mission-driven organizations. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the organizations we serve
- Showing empathy toward other community members and toward the people our work ultimately helps

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Trolling, insulting comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Misusing access to a charity's data or systems

## Responsible use of access

FDE work often means access to real systems and sometimes sensitive data about vulnerable people. Treat that access as a privilege. Use it only for the agreed scope, and follow [docs/data-and-privacy.md](docs/data-and-privacy.md).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.
